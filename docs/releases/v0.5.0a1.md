# PostEx™ v0.5.0a1 — Conference Intelligence foundations

PostEx can now describe where a poster is going before deciding how it should look. A year-specific Conference Pack joins official presentation facts, source provenance, verification state, independent PostEx recommendations, rights-safe Palette DNA, renderer tokens, and machine-readable preflight rules.

## Included Conference Packs

- CVPR 2026
- AACR Annual Meeting 2026
- ASCO Annual Meeting 2026
- ESMO Congress 2026
- RSNA Annual Meeting 2026
- AHA Scientific Sessions 2026
- ESC Congress 2026

Every pack includes Conference Safe, Balanced Fusion, and Visual Signature Palette DNA modes. No organizer logo or official template is bundled, and no endorsement is implied.

## Trust boundary

- Official rules require a source in the same annual edition.
- Organizer requirements and recommendations remain distinct from PostEx design guidance.
- Partially verified editions list their missing public fields explicitly.
- Exact PostEx fallback canvases never overwrite an unspecified official canvas.
- The renderer consumes only generic canvas, theme, component, and layout tokens; it never branches on a conference ID.

ASCO and ESC Congress ship with verified public 2026 technical scopes. AACR is verified for its public companion e-poster PDF path but not its physical board. ESMO, RSNA, and AHA ship as partially verified packs pending complete public 2026 presenter specifications.

## Alpha boundary

This release stabilizes data contracts, source handling, Palette DNA integration, and normalized-snapshot validation. Conference-native PPTX geometry, automatic artifact snapshot extraction, Trusted Export report merging, Research Archetypes, CLI catalog commands, and the remaining 30-pack catalog are scheduled in `TASKS.md`.
