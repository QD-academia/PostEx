# PostEx 0.3.0a1 — Built-in Palette Library

This prerelease adds a browsable, source-grounded library of 104 poster palettes:

- 19 Chinese city landmark cards using reusable public-domain or Creative Commons photographs;
- 50 university emblem cards following the 2026 ShanghaiRanking Chinese Universities Ranking;
- 35 authorized Genshin character cards, with five characters from each of Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan, and Nod-Krai.

Every card includes transparent source art, a six-role palette, color ratios, provenance, and redistribution-rights metadata. `postex palette-catalog --show-blockers` audits the full selection and reports 104 release-ready entries with zero blockers.

Release assets include the Python wheel and sdist plus a standalone palette archive and its SHA-256 checksum. The standalone archive contains all cutouts, extracted palette JSON, 900×1240 WebP cards, collection previews, source receipts, attribution, and a per-file hash manifest.

Third-party artwork remains subject to the rights recorded in `assets/palettes/rights.yaml` and is not relicensed under Apache-2.0.
