# Asset licenses and provenance

## Source paper and extracted figures

Zhang Y, Guo L, Dai Q, et al. “A signature for pan-cancer prognosis based on neutrophil extracellular traps.” *Journal for ImmunoTherapy of Cancer*. 2022;10:e004210. DOI: 10.1136/jitc-2021-004210.

The PDF and extracted figures are distributed under the article's CC BY-NC terms. They are included only for the non-commercial PostEx evaluation example, retain their original scientific colors, and are not covered by the repository's Apache-2.0 code license.

## Generated poster

The poster layout code is Apache-2.0. The generated poster remains subject to the source paper's CC BY-NC terms because it embeds the paper's figures and paraphrases its content.
